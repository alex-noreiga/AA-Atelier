Setup order

  1. Link invoice to order

    Set Order on the invoice, then confirm the order row shows it under Invoices. That is the side the site reads. One invoice per order.

  2. Deposit amounts

    First Deposit Amount, then Second Deposit Amount when you get there. Payable online the moment the number is above zero. No need to wait for the rest.

  3. Due dates

    Optional, and they are the only switch for reminder emails: First Deposit Due, Second Deposit Due, Payment Deadline (the balance).

  4. Itemize

    /studio → Itemize an invoice → order number. Or add lines by hand.

  5. Invoice Ready

    Reveals the balance and the invoice page. Everything before this point is deposits only.

Stage order does not matter

A stage is payable whenever it has an amount and is not marked paid. The balance nets whatever is marked paid at that moment, so second-before-first, or balance after one deposit, all reconcile.

Itemize tool

Reads the order's costing items and writes, at qty 1 with a manual unit price:

  • One Material line per usage line, at that line's material cost. Packaging usage lines skipped.
  • One Labor line, summed across costing items.
  • One Adjustment, "Design & finishing", which is the gap up to the costing's Suggested Price. The margin lives here.
  • One Surcharge line at 15% of that subtotal, if Rush Order is ticked on the order.

It also renames Invoice ID to the order number, and reports the total when it finishes.

  • Runs once. Any existing line item and it writes nothing. To rebuild, delete the lines first, then run it again.
  • Needs costing items on the order. No relation, no run.
  • Never link a Costing Item on a line you add by hand. That link outranks the manual price and pulls the whole-garment aggregate, so the material and labor lines end up billed twice.

Line types: Garment, Material, Labor, Adjustment, Surcharge. All five sum into the subtotal and show to the client under those headings. No Deposit type, and do not add one back.

Paid to Date, Remaining to Collect, and Payment Status are yours. The site reads none of them.

Gotchas

A ticked Paid box is the record of payment.
Tick it early and the client loses the pay button, and the balance drops by that amount. Untick to restore both.
The charge is not Final Balance.
The site sums the line items itself and subtracts paid deposits, floored at zero. Final Balance has no deposit filter, so a stray Deposit line would inflate your view while the charge stayed right.
Only paid deposits credit.
An unpaid one still lists on the client's invoice, at $0.00 and labeled unpaid.
Tax and installments are balance-only.
Sales tax is added at the balance checkout, which is why that one asks for a billing address. Klarna and Affirm show there too. Deposits are untaxed and card only.
Editing a line takes effect immediately.
No republish step. The next page load shows the new balance, unless the balance is already paid.

Reminders

Nightly, for any stage that is unpaid, has a due date, and is within seven days of it or already past. Email carries the amount and a pay link.

  • No due date, no reminder. That is the whole switch.
  • One per stage, then its Reminded box ticks. Untick for another.
  • Reconcile production milestones on /studio runs the same sweep on demand.

Symptom and fix

SymptomFix
Client cannot pay the balance Invoice Ready off, balance at or below zero, or Balance Paid already ticked.
Deposit card not showing Amount is blank or 0. Both are skipped.
Shows paid, nobody paid Untick that Paid box.
Itemize tool added nothing Lines already exist. Delete them and rerun.
Balance looks double A hand-added line is linked to a Costing Item. Clear the link or delete the line.
Paid by e-transfer, cash, in person Tick the stage's Paid box and put IN_PERSON in its Session Id. No receipt link will show, since there is no Stripe payment behind it.
Need to refund and cancel Cancel & refund an order on /studio. Covers both deposits and the balance, marks the order cancelled, emails the client. Safe to rerun.
Reminder went out too early or not at all Check the stage's due date and its Reminded box.
