- [Commission capacity & the waitlist](commission-capacity-waitlist.md) — roadmap card ③: when the books are full the intake form offers a **waitlist** instead of a bespoke commission (`GET /capacity`, `POST /waitlist`, a 409 gate on `POST /orders`, a `Commission capacity` panel on `/studio`). The card names the **Competitions** database as "the demand signal to read" and the first cut did read it — a client factory, schema, repository, env var and an event picker on the waitlist — all of which was **deleted on the atelier's call**: _"I'm not going to know about every single competition going on nationally and internationally."_ A curated event list is either a standing maintenance burden or a picker that silently omits the event the customer came for, and the customer knows theirs; the data agreed anyway, since every row in that database is undated, so anything computed from it would have shipped inert while looking correct. **Don't reintroduce it** — the waitlist asks two plain optional free-text fields (**"What's it for?"** — worded for skating AND dance, since the studio makes both — and when do you need it) and resolves neither, which gives the atelier the label and the date they actually need with nothing to keep current. Capacity is therefore just a number: `COMMISSION_CAPACITY` (default `0` = no cap) counted against commissions neither delivered nor cancelled, plus `COMMISSION_INTAKE` (checked FIRST, overriding the count in both directions); "seasonal" is the atelier setting those as their season demands. It **fails OPEN in every direction** (no cap / unusable cap / unreadable count), the opposite of `orderDelivered`, because a customer turned away by a Notion blip is gone silently; the count is `undefined` rather than `0` when the read fails (absent ≠ zero, as in the materials panel). **Only the bespoke commission is gated** (`capacityGated` in the service catalog, served on `GET /services`) — alterations/rhinestoning/repairs are hours on a piece already in hand, so they keep taking orders and the closed screen keeps the picker on show. `SERVICE_FALLBACK.capacityGated` is `true`, which is NOT the widest option: it degrades toward **matching the server**, since a serviceless order resolves to bespoke. The public endpoint carries **no counts** (anonymous — the numbers are behind the staff gate); the count is a **filtered** query, not the analytics' scan, and `Stage` is a **status** property in that filter. Seventh contact-inbox writer, **no new Notion property** (the event reuses the shared `Item` rich_text). **No setup of any kind.**

- [Order tracking — carrier tracking and local pickup](order-fulfilment-tracking.md) — roadmap card ① plus the gap raised with it: **local customers collect in person**, so their order has no tracking number and never will, and a permanently-empty tracking panel reads as a broken site. One `OrderFulfilment` shape now answers "where is my order?" on **both** `OrderStatus` and `ShopOrderStatus` (it **replaced** `ShopOrderStatus.tracking`, so there is one shape and one component), resolved by the pure `lib/fulfilment.ts` and rendered by `components/fulfilment-panel.tsx`. Custom orders finally read the four columns the card named (`Fulfilment`, `Ship By`, `Tracking Number`, plus a new `Delivery Method` / `Pickup Time` / `Pickup Location` trio shared with shop orders). The load-bearing rule is **a label with nothing behind it loses to a fact**: the declared method wins unless the order carries only the other kind's facts, which neutralizes a wrong default on the database template both ways; `Ship By` is deliberately NOT counted as a shipping fact (on a pickup order the atelier reads it as "ready by"). A pickup always shows (that it IS a pickup is the answer); a shipment shows only once there's a tracking number, ship-by date or handoff state. `shipBy` and the handoff state are dropped once shipped/delivered — a past "expected to ship by" reads as a broken promise — but the tracking number never is. **The customer's own address is read by neither repository** (the lookup is gated by order number alone); the pickup _location_ is the studio's, so it's safe. Corrects `order-stage-vs-fulfilment.md`: `Fulfilment` IS read now, as advisory shipping-leg copy only, never as completion. **No env var, no new database, no blocking setup** — three optional Notion properties per order database. The one **write**: a shop order paid with the atelier's local-pickup Stripe shipping rate marks itself, decided from the rate's **display name** (not its id — ids are mode-scoped, so pinning to one would mean a second env var to keep in step across two Stripe modes) through the same `looksLikePickup` the Notion select uses. That write lands on the webhook, which is why `createPageDroppingUnknownProperties` moved to `lib/notion/create-page.ts` and `createShopOrder` now shares it: a 400 over an un-added `Delivery Method` would lose a **paid** order, and the redelivery would fail identically.
